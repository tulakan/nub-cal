## Design Context

### Users
- **Who**: Individuals tracking their daily food intake, meal plans, and body metrics.
- **Context**: On-the-go usage, primarily on mobile phones in portrait orientation. Needs quick data entry and at-a-glance summaries.
- **Job to be done**: Easily log food, view remaining calories/macros, and track progress over time without visual clutter.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Clean, direct, and functional.
- **Tone**: Focused, calm, and efficient.
- **3-Word Personality**: Minimal, Precise, Functional.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: Modern Minimal.
- **Theme**: Monochrome (Black, White, Gray).
- **Key Constraints**: High information density for small screens. Avoid heavy cards and large gaps.

### Design Principles
1. **Space Efficiency**: Every pixel matters on mobile. Minimize padding and margins to maximize content display.
2. **Monochrome Clarity**: Rely on typography and spacing rather than color for hierarchy. Use color (like red/green) sparingly only for status indicators.
3. **Flattened Hierarchy**: Avoid nesting containers. Use simple lines or subtle background tints to separate sections.
4. **Mobile-First Touch Targets**: Ensure interactive elements are easy to tap (min 44px where possible) while keeping the overall layout compact.
